FRIDA: Fisheye Re-Identification Dataset with Annotations
Mertcan Cokbas, John Bolognino, Janusz Konrad, Prakash Ishwar — IEEE CVPR Workshop 2022
Introduces a first-of-its-kind dataset of 240k+ bounding-box annotations of people captured by overhead fisheye cameras. Evaluates 10 state-of-the-art person re-identification algorithms, showing up to 11.64% mAP improvement when training on FRIDA vs. standard rectilinear-camera datasets.
